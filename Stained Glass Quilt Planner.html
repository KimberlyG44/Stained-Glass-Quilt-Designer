Stained Glass Quilt Planner

Enter the finished size of your quilt and it lays out pieced blocks and sashing automatically, working around a center panel if you have one. Every measurement — block sizes, sashing, seam allowance — snaps to the nearest 1/4" so nothing ends up in an odd fraction you can't cut to.

Quilt Size

Sashing & Seams

Added to every side of every piece. Standard quilting seam is 1/4". You can type fractions like 3 1/2 or plain decimals like 3.5.
Two vertical seams from different parts of the design won't land closer than this, and same for two horizontal seams.

Block Sizes

Lower = boxier blocks (1 forces near-squares). Note the ceiling here is Max finished size ÷ Min finished size - set below that ratio and a few leftover pieces may still exceed it (you'll see a note after generating).
Seam allowance still snaps to 1/4" as usual - this rounds the blocks themselves to the nearest 1/2". Requires Quilt Size, Sashing width, and Panel size (if used) to be whole or half inches too, so every block can land on the grid cleanly.
Panel is centered on the quilt. Blocks and sashing are built in the four regions around it.

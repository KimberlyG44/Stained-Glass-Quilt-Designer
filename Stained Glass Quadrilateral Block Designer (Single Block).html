Stained Glass Quadrilateral Block Designer

Design a single block split by one straight line into two sections, with one or both sides optionally split again into 3 or 4 irregular quadrilateral pieces total - the classic random stained-glass patchwork look. Use it to plan a more elaborate block for the quilt planner, or on its own.

Block

This is the finished (sewn) size of the block, not the cut size - seam allowance is added automatically to each piece in the cutting list. You can type fractions like 6 1/2 or plain decimals. Use equal values for a square block.

Sashing & Seams

Runs between each pair of pieces inside the block.

Pieces

The block is split by one straight line into two sections. With 3 pieces, one section stays whole and the other splits again. With 4, both sections split again - each second cut crosses back through the first line, at a different spot on each side.
6" × 8"

Cutting list

Each piece is described by its four finished side lengths (in order around the shape) plus one diagonal, which together are enough to reconstruct the exact quadrilateral - then the same with seam allowance added around all four edges for cutting. All measurements round to the nearest 1/4".

Sashing strips run along the internal seam lines shown in the diagram. Cut strips at the sashing width plus seam allowance on both long edges, and trim the ends to match the angle where they meet each piece - this is the one spot that needs a little hand-fitting rather than a straight cut.
